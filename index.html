The uploader

The channel posts its videos with its own small app, Deadlock Replays Uploader. The app sends finished videos to this channel through the YouTube Data API. Only the channel's owner signs in to it. It asks Google for one permission: to upload videos.

By using Deadlock Replays Uploader, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How the app handles data is in the privacy policy.
